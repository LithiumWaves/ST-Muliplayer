Host a room
Playing over the internet, no VPN needed. Host from home Wi-Fi or broadband, not mobile data (carriers block incoming connections). With automatic port mapping ticked, the router opens the port and supplies your public address. Start hosting and look at Internet play below the code: it says whether that worked.

On a phone (Termux) the router often cannot be found automatically. Type its address in Router address (usually 192.168.1.1 or 192.168.0.1).

If the router will not open the port, forward it yourself in the router's settings (TCP, the port above, to the address shown under Internet play), then put your public IP or your router's DDNS hostname in Address players connect to. A Tailscale address or a tunnel hostname still works there too, if you ever want one.

Shared session
Only the chat of the character below, or the group chat the host opens, is shared. Messages in any other chat stay local to you, and nothing from the room can appear in them. To play a group, the host opens a SillyTavern group chat whose members are all shared; players then get the same group in their character list.
